Settings
Sound on/off; Performance, Balanced and Detail resolution; recenter; Back; Exit VR while in VR. Resolution changes do not select another splat file.

Desktop: click a 3D word, drag to look, R to recenter, Escape to go back. Quest: Enter VR, point a controller and pull the trigger. Outside the original photo, the surrounding shop is generated content. The scene works best near its standing viewpoint.
