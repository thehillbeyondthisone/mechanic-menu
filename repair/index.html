CONTROLS / HELP

Click objects; drag the drawer handle to pull it. Hold the mouse on the fault to inspect it. Drag empty space to look. E performs the current action. Space inspects. Escape docks a held object. R restarts.

Calibration: drag a physical knob sideways, or use its slider. Keys 1–3 select a channel; left/right arrows turn it. Make all three channels green and hold steady.

VR: point + trigger; squeeze to grab or pull. Hold trigger to scan. Squeeze a dial and twist to tune it. Trigger clicks provide small dial adjustments. The terminal RECENTER button realigns the scene.
